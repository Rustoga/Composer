Sensory Integration

Rustoga · album · released May 6, 2024 · electronic / ambient / vocal

Helsinki-based Rudolf ‘Rustoga’ Westerholm releases a dark, brooding electronic techno album titled “Sensory Integration,” which delves into sensory perceptions. The inspiration for the release of the 8-track album came from the Kuutonen club and a performance by Lil’ Tony there. “Being at the concert, where the music felt deep in my lungs and the only source of light was white, primitive recurring outlines (VJ Random Doctors & Klaustrofobia), strongly evoked the feeling of techno. Eventually, it was necessary to create something dark-roasted.”

“Sensory Integration” is self-produced, featuring 3 collaborations: MariusLurius on vocals and lyrics for the track “Vituttaa,” Albin as a DJ-producer on the track “Touch,” and Veikko Paasi as a vocalist on the track “Longing.”

The visual aesthetics of the album release mirror the taste of its live performance: white outlines, particle points in 3D structures, and a strong, dominant presence with immersion in the repetition of sound. Rustoga has crafted the songs, visual package, mixing, mastering, and production entirely by himself, excluding the collaborations.
