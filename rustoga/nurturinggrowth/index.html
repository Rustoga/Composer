About

Nurturing

Nurturing Growth 🌱 is meant for you to keep in the background when nurturing your plants or yourself in everyday tasks in life. My dear friend Joshio loves plants and has his own channel where he tends to them while going through his processes, so these were originally composed for him. The songs have been tied into helping him out in an allotment plot and enjoying doing with hands while being outside.

Hope you enjoy Nurturing Growth 🌱! Take care! Oh, and naturally (heh) no AI used.
